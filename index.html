The 3D radar changes everything

Other scanner apps give you a list. SignalRadar puts you inside the signal field.

  • 3D Bluetooth radar, tap a signal to lock on with touch and motion controls
  • Live bearing, distance and magnetic field readouts as you sweep your phone
  • Sonar ping and haptic pulses that intensify as you close in
  • Six radar palettes: Classic, Matrix, Amber, Ice, Infrared, Tactical

One app, every signal

The full feature list, tab by tab.

Scanner

  • 3D Bluetooth radar with tap to lock on and touch controls
  • Sonar ping and haptic pulses that intensify as you close in
  • AR Finder camera mode with floating signal orbs
  • List view: signal strength, estimated distance, device ID clues
  • Device detail: custom labels and per-device proximity alerts
  • Magnetic field readout in microtesla
  • Six radar palettes: Classic, Matrix, Amber, Ice, Infrared, Tactical

Map

  • Live heatmap for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, magnetic and EMF
  • Filter the heatmap by signal type
  • Walk sessions: start, save, and a history of past sessions

Wi-Fi

  • Current network info: name, router ID, band, signal
  • Signal AR: animated waves and direction to the strongest signal, GPS-logged points
  • Dead Zone Map: GPS heatmap, best used outdoors
  • Indoor Map: set floor size, paint walls, doors and stairs, tap to record readings, multiple floors
  • Ping, DNS lookup, traceroute, port scan and speed test

Tools

  • Beacon Decoder and Raw Advertisement Viewer
  • Tracker Scan: Apple Find My and AirTags, Tile, Samsung SmartTag, Chipolo, Google Find My Device trackers
  • Flags unknown devices that follow you across places, with a notification
  • LAN Scanner, Speed Test, Calibration and Diagnostics
  • Cellular Coverage: tower locations on a map and in AR, 5G/LTE/3G/2G filter, your own coverage samples

Export Center

  • Any dataset, or all of them: scan history, labels, radar plot, heatmap walks, Wi-Fi log, Dead Zone, towers, cellular samples, indoor plans
  • Formats: PDF, Word, JPEG, raw CSV/JSON/PNG, or everything in one ZIP
  • LAN Scanner, Wi-Fi log, Dead Zone, towers and Indoor Map each have their own share button

Settings and Privacy

  • Permission status at a glance
  • Alerts for device appear/disappear, tracker warnings and EMF threshold
  • Units, Simple/Expert mode, name tags, hide "Unknown" devices
  • Local-only privacy mode and Clear all app data
  • Dark UI throughout, splash screen, onboarding, and an in-app review prompt after three features used
