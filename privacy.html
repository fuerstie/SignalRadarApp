Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect nothing. Your scans never leave your iPhone.

Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. No data collection

SignalRadar does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party trackers in the app.

2. On-device processing

All scanning, signal analysis, heatmap rendering and device identification happen locally on your iPhone. Bluetooth scan results, walk sessions, labels and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Bluetooth: required to detect nearby Bluetooth devices. iOS does not allow Bluetooth scanning without it.
  • Location: required by iOS for Bluetooth scanning, and used to place your own signal readings on your personal heatmaps.
  • Camera: used only for the AR Finder mode, which overlays signal orbs on the live camera view. Images are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: used only for the alerts you enable, such as tracker warnings or proximity alerts.

Permission data is used solely for the features described above and never leaves your device.

4. Exports are yours

Exporting scan history (JSON/CSV) or custom labels only happens when you explicitly tap export, and the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it. We never see it.

5. Network features

Tools such as ping, DNS lookup, traceroute, port scan and speed test necessarily contact the network addresses you enter, exactly as any network utility does. Results stay on your device.

6. Clearing your data

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings inside the app, or by deleting the app, which removes its private storage from your iPhone.

7. Children

SignalRadar does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
